Question 10: cURL command and output showing the delete message after deleting a book review (logged-in user; session cookie saved at login with -c cookies.txt)

1) Delete own review for the book with ISBN 1 (after adding it, as in Question 9)

Command:

curl -s -X DELETE http://localhost:5000/customer/auth/review/1 -b cookies.txt

Output:

{"message":"Review for ISBN 1 deleted."}

2) Deleting a review without logging in (failure case)

Command:

curl -s -X DELETE http://localhost:5000/customer/auth/review/1

Output:

{"message":"User not logged in"}
